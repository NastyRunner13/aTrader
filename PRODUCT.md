# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

One investor doing personal research on NSE-listed companies, on a desktop, from a local app. They open a company, read its signal card, and want to know why each horizon scored the way it did: which rules moved the score, what evidence sits behind a claim, and what price would change the signal. They also start new research runs from the app and watch them progress. A phone should work for checking a result, but is not the main surface.

## Product Purpose

aTrader researches one NSE company at a time from official data and cites evidence for every claim. Code computes a 0–100 score and a signal for 1 month, 6 months and 2 years; language models only explain and adjust within small, verified limits. The web app is the reading and running surface for that work: search, the signal card, price chart with levels, run history, an evidence drawer, and a watchlist. It succeeds when the investor can trace any number on screen back to its source in one click, and never mistakes experimental scores for advice.

## Positioning

Every score on screen opens to the rules and the evidence that produced it.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, candid. Premium in the way a well-set research note is: restraint, tight type and calm density, not decoration. The tone is plain and sober, with the limits of the research stated on the surface.

## Anti-references

Not a brokerage or trading terminal: the app places no orders, so nothing should suggest urgency, flashing tickers or order tickets.

## Design Principles

- Show the why beside the what. A score, a signal or a claim is never a dead end; it opens to its rules and evidence.
- Colour is never the only cue. Every signal carries its word and its number.
- State uncertainty on the surface. The scores are experimental, ranges are not forecasts and levels are not targets, and the interface says so where the numbers are.
- Calm density over decoration. Tables, rules and type do the work; cards are used only where they earn it.
- Nothing spends the model allowance silently. A run shows what it will cost before it starts, and loading a page never starts one.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Signals do not rely on colour alone. Working baseline, not specified further by the user: WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard access, and reduced motion respected.
